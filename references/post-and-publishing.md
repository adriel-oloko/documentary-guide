# Post and publishing

Stage 10. One file, `post.txt`, and a handful of publishing rules.

## `post.txt` format

Four to six short paragraphs of caption, then a line of exactly five hashtags.

The caption states the hook, the correction and the consequence in plain language.
First person plural or impersonal voice. No question mark, no second person, no hype,
no call to action.

Real example, from the Vasa episode:

```
North of the harbour the water is shallow enough to wade, and a ship lay on the bottom for three hundred and thirty three years because she was built to a specification nobody would argue with.

The Vasa sailed about a thousand three hundred metres on her first voyage in sixteen twenty eight, heeled in a gust, took the Baltic through thirty open gun ports cut too close to the waterline, and went down inside Stockholm's own harbour with about a hundred and fifty people aboard. Between thirty and fifty of them died, in water the men on the shore could have swum out to.

The ship had already failed a stability test. Thirty men running side to side across the deck in January had rolled her so hard that the officer in charge stopped the exercise and said the men had all died if she went over. Nobody wrote it down. Nobody told the king, whose own order for a second deck of heavy guns on a hull drawn for one was the reason she was unstable. Seven months later she sailed anyway.

The inquest that autumn heard the evidence and concluded that the ship was built to the royal specification by men following orders. Nobody was punished.

Then the chemistry. The Vasa survived where every wooden wreck of her age is gone, because the Baltic is too cold and too brackish for the shipworm that eats hulls in every other sea.

#history #maritimeHistory #vasa #shipwrecks #documentary
```

Note three things about it. It opens on an image rather than a claim. It gives the
correction as narrative rather than as a statement of fact. And it does not tell the
viewer what to think.

## Hashtags

Exactly five. Lowercase or lower camel.

Start from a core pool and add one or two subject tags:

- Core: `#history #documentary #learnOnTikTok #storytime`
- Ships and wrecks: `#maritimeHistory #shipwrecks #navalHistory`
- Machines: `#engineering #warMachines`
- Animals: `#animals #history`

Never use a broad reach tag with no relation to the subject, and never
`#fyp`, `#viral`, `#historyfacts` or any variant that signals a content farm. They
attract the wrong audience, and the wrong audience is worse than no audience because it
tells the algorithm the film has no category.

## Short-form caption

At the SHORT shape the caption is one hook line plus two to four body lines telling the
story in micro without giving away the punchline, then the hashtag line. The hook line
is what shows before the more link, so it carries the whole click.

## Posting

- **Consistency beats perfect timing under a small following.** Two fixed slots a day in
  the audience's evening beats picking times by feel. Once there is enough data, move to
  what the data says.
- **Never delete and repost.** Re-uploads get suppressed.
- **Set the location to a major market** if the platform lets you, and route the upload
  through an endpoint in the market you are targeting, because the platform reads the IP
  at upload to set the initial distribution.
- **Two uploads a week for the first eight weeks, then weekly.** For a long-form
  documentary channel, a weekly cadence is more than the audience can absorb from a small
  channel anyway, and a fixed slot trains the returning viewer.
- **Order the slate, do not publish it at random.** The tiers in
  `idea-slate-example.md` are an audience building order, not a quality ranking.

## Reading the numbers

The diagnostics below are the ones worth acting on. They are about retention rather than
about views, because views without retention tell you nothing.

| Symptom | Diagnosis | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Average watch time under 10 seconds | Hook failure | Rewrite the opening line, lead with the number |
| Retention collapses at 0:01 | Opening visual does not match the audio | Give frame zero motion, and sync on screen text to the first words |
| Full watch under 5 percent | No pattern interrupts | Change the visual every 10 to 15 seconds |
| Zero comments | Consumable but not debatable | Sharpen the correction, or add the provocation ending |
| Zero follows from 500 or more views | No reason to stay | Open a series and say which part this is |
| Wrong country traffic above 20 percent | The route or the geo push failed | Fix the route. Reposting is not an option |
| Strong watch time, weak click rate | The thumbnail or the title | The subject was good and the cover was not |

## Titles and thumbnails

- The title carries the paradox, not the subject. The subject is already in the search
  index; the paradox is why somebody clicks.
- A thumbnail has one subject in it, at a size readable on a phone, and no text that
  repeats the title word for word.
- Do not put a face in the thumbnail on a channel where nothing else has faces.
- Generate the cover from the same prompt register as the episode. A cover in a different
  look is a promise the film does not keep.
