1. Two hundred men. That's roughly how many American soldiers were trapped in a pocket in the Argonne Forest in October 1918, surrounded by Germans and being shelled by their own side.
2. Their only way to call for help was a homing pigeon named Cher Ami. They pinned a message to her leg and threw her into the sky.
3. The Germans shot her through the breast. She fell. Then she got back up and kept flying. By the time she reached headquarters, she'd lost an eye, and one leg was hanging on by a tendon.
4. The message arrived. The shelling stopped. About 194 men walked out alive, and the army saved what was left of her with a tiny wooden leg.
5. She got a medal, and she got to retire. We love to say war is a human thing.
6. But a bird with a hole through its chest flew forty kilometers because a note was taped to her. She didn't know what the note said.
7. She just knew it mattered.
